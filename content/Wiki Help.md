# Knowledge and Lore

Some Wiki pages might sport callouts like so:

>[!abstract] Wiki Lore
>You understand that this is a Wiki for the Immortals campaign.

If your character has the requisite trait, feat, Knowledge skill, or is trained in the Skill presented, they know this particular piece of information by default. A good example is the Roil page: ![[Roil]]
Sometimes, these lore bits are denoted in footnotes[^1] instead, rather than interrupting the flow of the text. Keep an eye out for these.

You don't necessarily need to commit this knowledge to memory, but it's good to have an idea of what specifically your character knows about the world versus the others. 

[^1]: **Footnote Lore.** This is a footnote containing specific knowledge for those with the requisite Lore Skill, trait, or training.

# Unlinked Notes

Some proper nouns might not have links yet. This Wiki grows as the party encounters things, filling with knowledge between sessions.

You can find a list of added/updated notes in the `#wiki-updates` channel on the server. These come through as notifications every now and then, and show the top 5 of each (added vs updated).

# Backlinks and the Node Graph

On the right side of the screen is a Node Graph, displaying linked notes *from* this one, and Backlinks, which provide any notes that link *to* this one.

You can navigate to either the nodes or the backlinks by simply clicking them.

# Dark Mode / Reading Mode

You can toggle Dark/Light mode in the top left, as well as Reading Mode, which hides the sidebar, node graph, and backlinks when the mouse isn't hovering over them.
