---
title: Kingdoms
---

Each of the [[Immortals]] rules a kingdom in the [[Eldlands]], with their trade and other conquests sometimes overlapping.

- [[Behal]] rules the kingdom of [[Senessa]], with the capital city of [[K'thun]]
- [[Zakai]] rules the kingdom of [[Verdassa]], with the capital city of [[Aj'than]]
- [[Araxen]] rules the kingdom of [[Tordon]], with the capital city of [[Vendall]]
- [[Camelia]] rules the kingdom of [[Etiel]], with the capital city (and the rest of the kingdom) swallowed by the [[Dreaming Grotto]]
