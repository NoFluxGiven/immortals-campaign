A snowy, colder region in the [[Eldlands]], formerly ruled by [[Camelia]]. At its centre is the [[Dreaming Grotto]], a place where dreams and reality meld together, and made off-limits by the rest of the [[Immortals]].

Along the edges, small settlements exist, usually undernourished and frostbitten. Still, they live, and are happy to help those in need with what little they have.
