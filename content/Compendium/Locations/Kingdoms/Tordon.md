A swathe of rocky plateaus, mountains and long dormant volcanoes. Tordon is ruled by [[Araxen]]. Its capital is [[Vendall]], the "city that speaks", and the kingdom sports a number of libraries and institutions, the most famous of which being the [[Mercurial]], built into the side of a broken mountain and capturing lightning from nearby [[Ion Storm]]s.

Tordon's immense size and uneven terrain makes it one of the least densely populated regions, but villages and towns are scattered throughout. One thing you will always find there are libraries, sanctums and the varieties of heavily guarded [[Tordonian Archives]]. It shares a border with [[Senessa]], [[Behal]]'s domain.
