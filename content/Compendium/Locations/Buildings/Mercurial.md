A library in [[Tordon]]. It is carved into the side of the [[Sendon]], the kingdom's tallest mountain, and is frequently assailed by [[Ion Storm]]s. It has devices to absorb and redirect the lightning from these storms.

>[!abstract] Arcane Knowledge
>Spellbooks from ages past and other powerful historical artifacts are stored here. Many in [[Araxen]]'s orbit find themselves making a sort of erudite pilgrimage to the Mercurial, in the hopes of finding a greater understanding of their craft. The rumours though, speak of this place as a tightly controlled dispensary for knowledge, only allowing those most loyal to [[Araxen]] to access its repositories.
