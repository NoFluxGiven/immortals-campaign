>[!quote]
>Her sail raised
>It flutters high
>Luck, sailor's bade
>A seahawk cries
>They lurch 'pon Death
>for Teeth It bares
>and broken berth
>Lay sunken there
>> *Old sailor's rhyme*

So named for its underwater stone juts, and the subject of many colourful accounts, poems, and tall tales, the Teeth is a dangerous maritime obstacle that prevents normal ships from sailing westward through the [[Western Sea]], with only the reinforced [[Barges]] and smaller, more nimble boats able to navigate it.
