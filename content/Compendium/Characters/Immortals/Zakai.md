>Zakai ruled his kingdom with a thumb on the wax seal, exporting goods and controlling the words his populace heard, while waging secret wars and plundering resources without care. His [[Verdassa|kingdom]] was commerce, trade, whispered secrets and daggers in the necks of those who spoke too loud.

One of the four [[Immortals]]. He has filled his kingdom with aristocrats, merchants, and sycophants, valuing opulence and soft power above all else.

His [[Verdassa|kingdom]]'s symbol is the *hands*-  two hands crossing one another, one palm up and the other palm down, with an eye in its centre.
