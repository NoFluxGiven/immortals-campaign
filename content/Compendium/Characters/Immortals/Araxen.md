>Araxen was the oldest, and obsessively pursued the recording and collection of history, as it happened. He ruled gently, but his need for knowledge was so far reaching that he spurned his ilk, seeing them as too small minded and petty. His [[Vendall|kingdom]] was that of learning, and strength through knowledge, full of scholars, academics, wizards.

One of the four [[Immortals]]. He has filled his kingdom with scholars, wizards, and academics, and places emphasis on knowledge as power, and history as wisdom.

Araxen is known to poach those with magical acuity from the other kingdoms, even if they have committed treason or committed atrocities that normally cannot be overlooked.

The symbol of his kingdom is the *scroll* - a scroll with a line drawn down its centre.
