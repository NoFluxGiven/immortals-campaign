The Novians are an ancient tribe, originally responsible for the [[Scripture|Eldscript]]. They operate in nearly all of the [[Kingdoms of the Immortals|major kingdoms]]. They wear yellow fabric to signify their rank.

Most Novians are proselytisers and advisors directly to the [[Immortals]], spreading the word of [[Scripture|Eldscript]] to those who would listen, and acting as priests and priestesses of the [[Faith]].
