Also known as the **Eldscript**, the Scripture is a set of religious texts derived from the [[Immortals]] and their advisors, the [[Novian]]s' experience with the [[Elder Gods]].

It details the rise of the [[Immortals]] and their various conquests, alongside serving as a guide for the populace, encouraging obedience.

The Scripture defines the [[Immortals]] as the "new gods" of the [[Eldlands|Mainland]] and the world beyond. It is the primary religion of the [[Eldlands|Mainland]] and the only religion that is practised in public, with severe penalties befalling those who worship the [[Old Pantheon]]s.

Many practitioners refer to the [[Immortals]] as the "benevolence", and the Scripture encourages daily or weekly worship, placing mortals as crucial but flawed creatures of sin.
