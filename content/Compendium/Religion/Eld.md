The God that came before the [[Old Pantheon|Gods]], also known as the **Primeval**, **The First** or the **Demiurge**. Written in the [[Scripture]] as "a great, fracturing shadow", the **Eld** is the only one of its kind placed in the [[Eldlands]]' history.

According to the [[Scripture]], in a place outside of time, this deity imbued the [[Immortals]] with their immortality, tasking them and the [[Novian]]s with carrying the torch of godhood and becoming the [[New Gods]]. In the same verse, it labels the [[Old Pantheon]] - a set of gods that rose after the Eld, but before the [[Immortals]] - illegitimate, as the Eld never passed on its blessing to them.
