Huge steel colossi that cut through the waters of the [[Western Sea]]. They are designed solely for the purpose of housing hundreds of [[Banished]], and have little in the way of comforts for the 6 - 8 month long journey. Once they reach the [[Roil]], they are often in dire need of repairs, having toed the sharp stones in the [[Teeth]][^2].

On board is generally a small crew of repairmen and engineers, and a large number of guards[^1].

>[!info] Qualities
>Looming tall at around 48 ft, a Barge is a structural marvel architecturally, but thrown together hastily by underpaid and overworked labourers. Each plate weighs multiple tonnes, and was hauled into place by man and machine before being welded into the main hull by a [[Hearthmage]].
>
>The shape of the barge tapers to a sheer, bladelike bow, allowing it to cut through the waters with ease.

[^1]: **Underworld Lore**. They are not often paid a wage in real terms - many of the guards are instead absolved of prior crimes, and placed on the [[Barges]] as a form of service.

[^2]: **Sailor's Lore**. The Teeth are especially deadly for wooden ships, and considered unsailable by normal means. Even with steel reinforcements, these ships were clearly never designed with the guarantee they'd reach their destination safely.
