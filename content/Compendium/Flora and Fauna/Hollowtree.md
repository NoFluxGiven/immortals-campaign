Rare on the [[Eldlands|Mainland]], but found in great numbers at the water's edge on the [[Isles]].

>[!abstract] Survival Knowledge
>These trees hold water within them, but their trunks are nearly as hard as steel. Breaking into one would require a great deal of effort.

>[!abstract] Arborist Knowledge
>These trees are parasites. They sit at key thresholds between biomes, and drain the water and nutrients from nearby plants, choking them out until no others remain. They are generally a sign that vegetation here will be sparse and unyielding.
