title: Building Characters
---
Building a character in this setting might be challenging, as there are a few major questions to answer, there are some class restrictions in place, and your character will be inhabiting a world different from the one they are used to.

As such, it's important that your character has some level of history to them - ties to the [[Eldlands|Mainland]], family, friends, co-workers, brothers-in-arms, fellows, and the like. When they become [[Banished]], they are taken away from all of this.

The following notes are meant to guide you through a process of

- Building a history for them
- Creating a character that meshes well with the existing setting, and
- Creating a character that can survive the harsh (but beautiful) lands of the [[Isles]]. 

The main questions you should be able to answer (or eventually be able to) for your character are:

- Where did they live?
- Who were they on the [[Eldlands|Mainland]]?
- What caused their [[Banished|banishment]]?
- What pushes them to survive and persist through these hardships?
- In their worst moments, what brings them hope?

# Class Rules

## Very Rare Classes
*0 - 1 in the party, leaning towards 0 - discuss with your DM*

- **The *Psychic***. Some escaped experiment of [[Behal]]'s, this class is vanishingly rare. There are terrible consequences for both the Psychic and its creators, and no one would understand their abilities, outside of [[Behal]]'s most loyal subjects.

## Rare Classes
*0 - 2 in the party - discuss with your DM*

- **Religious classes** like the *Oracle*, *Cleric*, and *Champion* are exceedingly rare. Whatever gods they worship are long forgotten, and only a tiny spark remains, meaning their abilities are hampered significantly.
- **Most spellcasting classes**, including the *Sorceror*, *Wizard*, *Necromancer* and *Alchemist*, are not likely to find themselves on the [[Barges]]. Between the [[Immortals]], they are some of the most sought after of professions, with [[Araxen]] poaching the soon-to-be-[[Banished]] all the way up until they day they are shipped off.

## Uncommon Classes
*No limit, but discuss with your DM*

- **Some Occult and mixed classes**, like the *Thaumaturge* and the *Witch*. They are outsiders, not trusted by the people, and considered strange and blasphemous by those who worship the [[Scripture]].
- ***Druids* and *Summoners***. Similar to the Thaumaturge and the Witch, these are outsiders, though for different reasons - they worship spirits and the earth itself, and their philosophies are alien to the people who believe in the Immortals as gods.
